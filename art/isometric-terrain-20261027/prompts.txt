01-forest-meadow — 森林草甸
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one original 2D isometric terrain tile PNG for a strategy game.
Input image: the attached image is a visual STYLE AND TILE GEOMETRY REFERENCE only. Create new terrain artwork.
Primary request: Create ONE isolated diamond-shaped terrain tile, with the same pleasing hand-drawn, dark-ink-outlined, muted-color strategy-map visual language as the reference.
Composition: landscape 1536x1024 canvas. A single complete flat 2:1 rhombus ground footprint viewed from a fixed orthographic isometric camera, horizontal long diagonal, approximately the following matching anchors in every tile of this set: left ground corner (64,608), top ground corner (768,256), right ground corner (1472,608), bottom ground corner (768,960). Keep all four ground corners clear and complete and the whole asset inside the canvas with transparent padding. Elevated natural features may rise above the back ground edge but do not extend outside the canvas. Ground has a fine dark boundary and absolutely NO thick block base, NO extruded floating island, NO vertical soil slab. Draw landscape detail at the same map scale as the reference, with many small readable details and broad quiet areas, not a close-up diorama.
Style: expressive clean dark charcoal-brown ink contours, irregular organic shapes, flat painted color regions with restrained two- or three-tone cel shading, subtle illustrated texture. Muted sage and olive foliage, earthen brown terrain, blue-green water where present. Warm diffuse light from upper left. Stylized overhead game map illustration with clear silhouettes, no photorealism, no rendered 3D plastic look.
Backdrop: actual transparent alpha everywhere outside the illustrated tile silhouette; no painted checkerboard or backdrop, no background shadow.
Constraints: ONE TILE ONLY, no collage, no sheet, no neighboring tiles, no characters, no resource tokens, no roadsigns, no badges or UI, no text, no numbers, no logo or watermark. Original artwork inspired by the broad reference aesthetic.
Subject: A lush green woodland and open meadow tile. A loose grove of small rounded deciduous trees, a few slender conifers, mossy layered slate rocks and grass clumps, with a winding earthy footpath through an open grassy clearing. Varied greens and warm brown earth. Rolling modest terrain, airy space between trees, modest height.
